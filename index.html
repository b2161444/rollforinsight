How to play

  1. Enter a character name for each role.
  2. Take turns rolling two digital dice.
  3. When a roll reaches or passes a halt, stop on that halt and complete its prompt.
  4. Your token stays on the halt after the challenge.
  5. All four players must complete Cursed Treasure to unlock the center.
  6. Work together to defeat the Final Boss!

Teacher controls

Teacher-editable prompts are in the PROMPTS object near the top of the JavaScript.
