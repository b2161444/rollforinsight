How to play

  1. Enter a character name for each role.
  2. Players take turns rolling two dice and moving along their path.
  3. Each party quest only needs to be completed once. Later players pass completed quest stops.
  4. At Cursed Treasure, enter the final two sentences and get party agreement.
  5. All four players must reach the end of their path before the Final Boss unlocks.
  6. At the Final Boss, enter the final response and follow the D20 challenge.

Teacher controls

Party quest progress is shared: once a halt is completed, every player may pass it.
